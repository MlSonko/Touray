Hello and welcome to The Gambia

My name is Muhammed Jobarteh, founder of Medtoursgambia.

For five years, i worked as a tour guide under a category B tour operator in The Gambia. This experience allowed me to meet visitors from different parts of the world and Discover more about my beautiful country.

I created Medtoursgambia to give visitors a more authentic way to experience The Gambia, while supporting local communities, schools and businesses.

Whether you want to discover our culture and history, explore nature, enjoy our beaches or experience The Gambia through local eyes, i would be happy to welcome you.

I look forward to welcome you to The Gambia.

ROOTS TOUR(KUNTA KINTEH ISLAND)

This trip starts from your hotel from where we will drive to join others at the Banjul seaport and cruise to Kunta kinteh island.On arrival at juffureh you be taken to see the freedom pole, You will see and feel the warmth welcome from the local people as you walk through the craft market. Finally we take a boat to the island, The boat cruise for 15minutes, You will be educated about the island take pictures and back to the boat. Then back to the resturant where lunch will be served before heading back to Banjul seaport. The transport will be waiting.

  • Meseum
  • Slave Trade History
  • Boat Ride To The Island
  • Food and drinks, entry tickets all included

NORTH SENEGAL TOUR(FATHALA)

This is a full day where we pick you from your hotel and drive to banjul ferry terminal. This, in itself, is quite an experience as you join the hustle and bustle of everyday life on the crossing to senegal. Back to the land rover and on to the Fathala wildlife reserve where you get to see rhinoes, giraffe, zebras, antelopes and many more. Having a good camera for photos would be nice. The whole journey there and back gives you a unique insight into life as it is in both the Gambia and senegal.

  • Ferry Crossing
  • Land Rover Rides
  • Fathala Wildlife
  • Local Village
  • Food and drinks, entry tickets all included

BANJUL HIGHLIGHTS

This is an orientation tour that takes you around the capital of The Gambia. You will stop at the ARCH 22, which is our national monument, From where you will have a good view of banjul. we will then drive to the Albart market where there are range of food and clothes etc and is full of Gambian women going about their business in colourful Gambian dress. The final visit will be to the biggest hospital build since the colonial time. We will lunch at the restaurant next to the beach.

  • Banjul Tour
  • Albert Market
  • Hospital Visit
  • Food and drinks entry tickets all included
