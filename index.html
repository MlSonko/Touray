Hello and welcome to The Gambia

My name is Muhammed Jobarteh, founder of Medtoursgambia.

For five years, I worked as a tour guide under a category B tour operator in The Gambia. This experience allowed me to meet visitors from different parts of the world and discover more about my beautiful country.

I created Medtoursgambia to give visitors a more authentic way to experience The Gambia, while supporting local communities, schools and businesses.

Whether you want to discover our culture and history, explore nature, enjoy our beaches or experience The Gambia through local eyes, I would be happy to welcome you.

I look forward to welcoming you to The Gambia.

ROOTS TOUR (KUNTA KINTEH ISLAND)

This trip starts from your hotel from where we will drive to join others at the Banjul seaport and cruise to Kunta Kinteh island. On arrival at Juffureh you will be taken to see the freedom pole. You will see and feel the warm welcome from the local people as you walk through the craft market. Finally we take a boat to the island, the boat cruise takes 15 minutes. You will be educated about the island, take pictures and go back to the boat. Then back to the restaurant where lunch will be served before heading back to Banjul seaport. The transport will be waiting.

  • Museum
  • Slave Trade History
  • Boat Ride To The Island
  • Food and drinks, entry tickets all included

NORTH SENEGAL TOUR (FATHALA)

This is a full day where we pick you up from your hotel and drive to Banjul ferry terminal. This, in itself, is quite an experience as you join the hustle and bustle of everyday life on the crossing to Senegal. Back to the Land Rover and on to the Fathala wildlife reserve where you get to see rhinos, giraffe, zebras, antelopes and many more. Having a good camera for photos would be nice. The whole journey there and back gives you a unique insight into life as it is in both The Gambia and Senegal.

  • Ferry Crossing
  • Land Rover Rides
  • Fathala Wildlife
  • Local Village
  • Food and drinks, entry tickets all included

BANJUL HIGHLIGHTS

This is an orientation tour that takes you around the capital of The Gambia. You will stop at Arch 22, our national monument, from where you will have a good view of Banjul. We will then drive to Albert Market where there is a range of food and clothes and it is full of Gambian women going about their business in colourful Gambian dress. The final visit will be to the biggest hospital built since colonial times. We will have lunch at the restaurant next to the beach.

  • Banjul Tour
  • Albert Market
  • Hospital Visit
  • Food and drinks, entry tickets all included
